Buyback Policy

1. What you buy. Each pack is the purchase of one authentic, physical trading card from our serialized, insured inventory. The card you receive is assigned at checkout using the odds and card value ranges shown before you tap Buy. The card you buy is the card you receive.

2. Market value source. Card values are current US-dollar market prices for the listed condition, sourced from TCGplayer market data (via pokemontcg.io) and PriceCharting sold listings; graded slabs use the sold price for that grader and grade. How RipZone works

Priced in US dollars

Add funds to buy packs. Optional buybacks are paid as Store Credit for more packs; Store Credit is never cashed out. No coins and no conversion.

📦 Real cards, three choices

Every pulled card is a real card valued at its current market price. Keep it in your Vault, request to have it shipped to you, or sell it back for 100% of market value in store credit to you (flat fee, placeholder in this demo).

The basics

  • Every pack shows its odds and value range before you buy. Tap Odds & Rules on any pack or on the reveal screen for exact odds, expected value, the guaranteed floor and the top card.
  • Expected value 88%. The odds are set so the average market value of the card in a pack (every pack holds one) is 88% of its price. The value spreads (Normal, High, Max) change the spread, not the average. Every card is worth at least the pack's floor (10–40% of the price) in these modes. Normal: about half of pulls are worth the price or more (about 40% on the $1 and $2,500 packs). High: about 1 in 5 pulls are worth the price or more, with more top cards and more low-value cards. Max: a 10% chance of a Gold card worth 3× the price or more (about 5× on average), otherwise a card worth less than the price (about 0.4× on average). 50/50: Gold (about 43%) is a card worth 1.8–2.2× the price, Silver is a card worth 0.05–0.11× (90% payback).
  • Card values are market prices. Raw near-mint (ungraded) market prices from TCGplayer, Scryfall, optcgapi.com and SportsCardsPro; PSA 10 entries use the PSA 10 sold price and carry a label. Each card shows its source and price date.
  • Vault cards are held 5 days. Ship a card to keep it. After 5 days, unshipped cards auto-sell at the 100% buyback as Store Credit; each card shows a countdown.
  • Practice first: new collectors get 3 free practice rips. They're scripted showcases, cost nothing and never change your balance.
  • 18+ only. Availability depends on where you live (to be set by counsel).
  • Buy responsibly. Set spending, deposit and session limits or take a cool-off on the Responsible Use page.
